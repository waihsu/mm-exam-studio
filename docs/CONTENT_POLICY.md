# Content and Copyright Policy

Only publish question content that the contributor owns, has permission to use, or is clearly licensed for redistribution. Record the source, grade, subject, language, and any adaptation notes during review.

Do not copy private school papers, paid coaching material, answer keys, student work, or copyrighted textbooks into the public question bank unless explicit permission covers publication and reuse.

Questions must be reviewed for factual accuracy, unambiguous expected answers, suitable language, and accessibility. Published questions are subject to automated readiness checks, but those checks do not replace subject-matter review.

If you believe content violates copyright or contains a material error, report it privately to a maintainer with the question reference, source evidence, and requested remedy.
