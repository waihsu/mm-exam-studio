# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-03

MM Exam Studio stores the information needed to provide a signed-in learning workspace: account profile and session information, practice activity, generated question papers, support messages, and device-local drafts or temporary PDF previews.

We use this information to authenticate users, synchronize their learning work, generate papers and previews, operate support, prevent abuse, and maintain the service. Temporary PDF files and local drafts may remain on a device until cleared by the app, sign-out flow, or the user.

Do not upload private student work, copyrighted exam material, or content you are not authorized to share. Public question content must follow the [Content and Copyright Policy](CONTENT_POLICY.md).

The project should publish this policy at a stable public URL before opening access to learners. The repository owner must add the actual data-controller contact, hosting region, retention period, and applicable legal jurisdiction for the deployed service.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the private support contact configured by the repository owner. Do not post account information or personal data in a public issue.
