# Code of Conduct

MM Exam Studio welcomes contributors, educators, and learners from every background. Be respectful, assume good intent, give useful feedback, and protect learner privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized language, plagiarism, or sharing private data are not acceptable. Content that targets or harms students is not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other proportionate action to keep the community safe.
